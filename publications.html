2024 · Technical report

Phi-3 Technical Report: A Highly Capable Language Model Locally on Your Phone​

Marah Abdin et al. (including Amin Saied)

Introduces compact language models trained on curated and synthetic data, with Phi-3-mini demonstrating strong benchmark performance while remaining small enough to run locally on a phone.

2024 · Findings of NAACL

AGIEval: A Human-Centric Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models​

Wanjun Zhong, Ruixiang Cui, Yiduo Guo, Yaobo Liang, Shuai Lu, Yanlin Wang, Amin Saied, Weizhu Chen, Nan Duan

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: NAACL 2024, pages 2299-2314.

Introduces a benchmark based on human examinations to evaluate foundation models' knowledge, reasoning, and problem-solving abilities, revealing where strong overall performance masks specific limitations.

2018 · PhD thesis

Stable representation theory of categories and applications to families of (bi)modules over symmetric groups​

Amin Saied

Develops a framework for representation stability in families of symmetric-group bimodules, introducing PD-modules and connecting their theory to tensor algebras and the homology of partition lattices.

Amin Saied

Turns the decomposition of tensor powers of free associative algebras into combinatorial counting problems, extending the range of computable representation-theoretic coefficients by more than 750-fold.
