<p className="eyebrow">2024 &middot; Technical report</p>

## [Phi-3 Technical Report: A Highly Capable Language Model Locally on Your Phone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14219)

<p className="authors">Marah Abdin et al. (including Amin Saied)</p>

<p className="publication-summary">
Introduces compact language models trained on curated and synthetic data, with
Phi-3-mini demonstrating strong benchmark performance while remaining small enough
to run locally on a phone.

<section className="publication">
<p className="eyebrow">2024 &middot; Findings of NAACL</p>

## [AGIEval: A Human-Centric Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.149/)

<p className="authors">Wanjun Zhong, Ruixiang Cui, Yiduo Guo, Yaobo Liang, Shuai Lu, Yanlin Wang, Amin Saied, Weizhu Chen, Nan Duan</p>

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: NAACL 2024,
pages 2299-2314.

<p className="publication-summary">
Introduces a benchmark based on human examinations to evaluate foundation models'
knowledge, reasoning, and problem-solving abilities, revealing where strong
overall performance masks specific limitations.
</p>

<p className="publication-links">[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.149/) &middot; [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.149.pdf) &middot; [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06364)</p>
</section>

<section className="publication">
<p className="eyebrow">2018 &middot; PhD thesis</p>

## [Stable representation theory of categories and applications to families of (bi)modules over symmetric groups](https://core.ac.uk/download/pdf/196232395.pdf)

<p className="authors">Amin Saied</p>

<p className="publication-summary">
Develops a framework for representation stability in families of symmetric-group
bimodules, introducing PD-modules and connecting their theory to tensor algebras
and the homology of partition lattices.
</p>

<p className="publication-links">[Read the thesis](https://core.ac.uk/download/pdf/196232395.pdf)</p>
</section>

<section className="publication">
<p className="eyebrow">2017 &middot; Mathematics</p>

## [An efficient algorithm computing composition factors of $T(V)^{\otimes n}$](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04326)

<p className="authors">Amin Saied</p>

<p className="publication-summary">
Turns the decomposition of tensor powers of free associative algebras into
combinatorial counting problems, extending the range of computable
representation-theoretic coefficients by more than 750-fold.
</p>

<p className="publication-links">[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04326)</p>
</section>

<section className="publication">
<p className="eyebrow">2016 &middot; Mathematics</p>

## [On the FI-module structure of $H^i(\Gamma_{n,s})$](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05861)

<p className="authors">Amin Saied</p>

<p className="publication-summary">
Uses FI-modules to sharpen representation-stability bounds for the cohomology of
groups arising from graph homotopy equivalences, and shows that their characters
eventually follow polynomial formulas.
</p>

<p className="publication-links">[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05861)</p>
</section>
